How Overcenter works

The kernel keeps known execution rules deterministic and leaves unresolved judgment to an agent or operator.

inspect authoritative facts
        ↓
derive READY work
        ↓
claim @ exact revision
        ↓
reserve effect durably
        ↓
execute uncertain action
        ↓
observe authoritative reality
        ↓
verify exact postcondition
        ↓
settle durable evidence
        ↓
recompute project projection ↺

Responsibility boundaries

Deterministic software
Reconciliation, evidence checks, retry, and recovery when the rules are known.
Reasoning agent
Investigates cases that are not yet reducible to deterministic rules.
Human operator
Handles decisions that remain outside the automated boundary.

Generated-site boundary

The generated site is read-only. Removing it does not change admission, execution, evidence, recovery, or settlement behavior.
